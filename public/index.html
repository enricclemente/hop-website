01Who we are

Two senior partners. Forty years of leaps. Twelve together.

We both married into Poland, and stayed for what we found: world-class products, and almost no one telling the story.

Philip Jacobson

Senior partner

Philip was Chief Brand Officer at PE-backed Vimergy.

For the last five years he has driven brand-led growth at scale-ups across categories, including Tylko, Autry and Chilly’s. Before that, he spent a decade leading brand communication for Nike EMEA and was Global Head of Brand Creative at Maserati.

David Anson

Senior partner

David was a Brand Creative Lead at Nike and Head of Business Leadership and Client Service at BBH Singapore.

Across BBH Singapore, Wieden+Kennedy Amsterdam and Saatchi & Saatchi London, he has led the Nike, Samsung, Meta, Netflix and HSBC business.

Now they bring that standard to Poland.

02What we do

Everything the leap needs. One partner.

08 services

  1. Find out what everyone thinks of your brand, from leadership to entry-level employees to customers. Finding the gaps and redefining it from the inside out first is non-negotiable. It makes everything else work.

  2. Anchor your brand strategy in the customer: understand their needs deeply and solve their problems better than anyone else.

  3. A clear customer value proposition that makes you the only choice in your market.

  4. A strategy for localising and globalising who you are, so you can win new markets and recruit new audiences.

  5. A single, memorable idea, expressed across every touchpoint, that becomes your identity and drives growth.

  6. Every message and experience, aligned to one consistent voice that builds coherence and trust.

  7. A framework that directs your effort and energy, focusing every action on what delivers competitive advantage and galvanises your team.

  8. Brand and performance optimised in real time, cutting inefficient spend and maximising effectiveness.
